Angled Landing Gear

2025 Competition Drone

Designed the drone's landing gear for the 2025 competition with the following features:

  • Angled legs to ensure concentricity with a water retrieval bucket
  • Parametric and modular design for two payload configurations
  • Integration with existing machined components for cost and time efficiency
  • Key features like angled portions, carbon fiber members, anti-tipping bars, and depth limiters

How

Created parametric models in SolidWorks accessible for team collaboration. Used hand calculations to validate angles, structural strength, and integrity.

Why

Leveraged parametric modeling and collaborative platforms for efficient design iterations. Implemented design choices like carbon fiber for rigidity, anti-tipping bars for stability, and depth limiters for precise bucket alignment.

Results

Fully manufactured landing gear demonstrated stability, concentricity with the bucket, and reliability during flight tests, with anti-tipping bars saving the drone from tipping in three out of four flights.
